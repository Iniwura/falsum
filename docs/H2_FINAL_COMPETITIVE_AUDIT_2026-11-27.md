# FALSUM — final competitive audit (27 November 2026)

**Status: NOT SUBMITTED.** This is an evidence-based readiness audit, not an
organizer score and not a claim of first place. The optional Virtuals ACP
integration is kept separate so it cannot delay a truthful Evaluators entry.

## Official bar

The current [Season II rules](https://hackathon.telegraphprotocol.com/rules)
define Evaluators as an evaluation adapter, evaluation material, or both, for a
Telegraph Intent. Every entry must be real and independently testable, show a
real Telegraph integration, repeatable results, safe handling, accurate claims,
and the required bounties-repository star. The current client-rendered
Evaluators rubric is **40 measurement validity/baseline (minimum 28), 15
integrity, 10 reproducibility/protocol compatibility, 10 useful coverage and
demonstrated improvement, 5 documentation/integration, and 20 organic growth**;
the published overall threshold is 70/100. The exact extraction is pinned in
[the Evaluators rubric note](H2_EVALUATORS_RUBRIC_2026-11-27.md).

For Evaluators delivery awards, the rules prioritize distinct commercial
missions, distinct H2 workflows, independently verified missing mission-critical
Intent needs, and combined technical quality. Evaluator excellence ties then
prefer proven measurement improvement or valuable new coverage, followed by
validity. FALSUM should therefore lead with reproducible new coverage and
measurement safeguards, not with an unverified ACP transaction.

## Phase 5BJ fresh public-provider holdout

The [frozen protocol](../data/phase5bj-fresh-provider-holdout-protocol.json)
selected two exact paths from public provider-tree metadata before either
response body was inspected. The [source-authenticated implementation](../src/falsum/phase5bj_fresh_provider_holdout.py)
then verified the provider Git blobs and the pinned GitHub Advisory Database
records without returning raw response bodies, payment objects, receipts, or
tokens.

The unseen Django 3.2.0 response emitted 66 identifiers. Thirty-eight mapped
to canonical GitHub-reviewed advisory records at the pinned commit; 27 were
applicable to the exact PyPI target and 39 remained unknown/insufficient and
were not forced positive. The unseen lodash 4.18.1 response emitted zero
advisories. An independently implemented numeric version-interval baseline
agreed with FALSUM on all 66 rows, including explicit abstention behavior.

This adds genuine public-provider coverage and reproducibility. It does not
claim a fresh accuracy gain, recall, security clearance, human AppSec gold,
current advisory completeness, customer adoption, ranking improvement, or a
new paid Telegraph execution. The [contract suite](../tests/test_phase5bj_fresh_provider_holdout.py)
checks source/target tampering, fixed/last-affected boundaries, unsupported
ranges, and sensitive-field exclusion. The [hosted workflow](../.github/workflows/phase5bj-fresh-provider-holdout.yml)
is the authoritative CI path for this study.

The [final judge experience](H2_FINAL_JUDGE_EXPERIENCE_2026-11-27.md) puts this
fresh coverage beside the prior Phase 5BI bounded 2/12 measurement, with the
[strict score](H2_FINAL_STRICT_SCORE_2026-11-27.md) and [submission packet](H2_FINAL_SUBMISSION_PACKAGE_2026-11-27.md)
keeping all owner and external-verification gaps explicit.

## Phase 5BM independent balanced holdout

The new [frozen protocol](../data/phase5bm-independent-balanced-holdout-protocol.json)
uses six previously unused package families, two first lexicographic
GitHub-reviewed advisories per package, immutable raw advisory blobs at a pinned
Git commit, and one conservative predecessor/exact-boundary pair per advisory.
It fixes the case roster and eligibility rules before response construction.
The 24 cases are balanced 12/12 by answer order and rendered with equal-length
surfaces so identifier-only, package-only, and answer-position shortcuts are
measured rather than assumed away.

The [result](../data/phase5bm-independent-balanced-holdout-result.json) is
24/24 for FALSUM, 24/24 for a separately implemented competent interval
baseline, and 12/24 for citation-only. Thus the new holdout removes 12
citation-only false positives while preserving the competent-baseline tie.
The [adversarial tests](../tests/test_phase5bm_independent_balanced_holdout.py)
cover frozen protocol integrity, source tampering, withdrawn/unsupported
records, and shortcut leakage. A first attempt to pin live OSV API bytes was
rejected when response bytes changed between requests; the final protocol uses
immutable GitHub Advisory Database blobs with exact hashes. This is a stronger
reproducibility and discrimination result, not human AppSec gold, full recall,
or a claim of superiority over the competent baseline.

## Phase 5BN correction

The historical Phase 5BM result is preserved but no longer treated as the
current perfect measurement. Phase 5BN removes the former FALSUM-dependent
boundary/layout risk: it freezes actual PyPI/npm registry versions, derives
reference labels with `packaging==26.4` and `semver@7.8.6` before the candidate,
uses a fixed label-blind seed, and verifies all claimed versions still exist.
It evaluates 48 target-specific competing answers and computes leakage tests for
case index, answer position and answer token.

The corrected result is 22/24 FALSUM versus 24/24 for the external resolver.
The two unknown disagreements are published. The answer surfaces are
meaningful, and the clean protocol shows no leakage signal at the predeclared
permutation threshold while adversarial index/token mutations are detected.
This is stronger validity evidence but not human AppSec gold or a FALSUM win
over the competent version resolver.

## Phase5BO semantic improvement and production check

The [Phase5BO protocol](../data/phase5bo-ecosystem-semantics-protocol.json)
keeps six raw registry responses and their SHA-256 sidecars in the repository,
so replay does not depend on a live PyPI/npm response remaining unchanged. The
candidate uses ecosystem-native PEP 440 and strict SemVer semantics, supports
disjoint intervals, and abstains on unsupported ranges. It resolves the two
Phase5BN regressions and adds 12 unseen cases, including four prereleases.
Structured claims/actions are selected from candidate output and then scored;
reference labels never choose the answer token. The bounded result is 14/14
candidate decisions and 14/14 structured claim/action selections. The option
prose is display-only, not natural-language answer scoring. No points are
added automatically, and this does not establish external validation.

The dated [production experience check](../evidence/phase5bo-production-experience-check-2026-11-27.json)
read-only verified the public witness and applications routes at HTTP 200 with
public-safe response checks. It does not claim that Phase5BO is deployed or

## Competitive conclusion

FALSUM has a credible technical-material entry and a differentiated safety story:
it makes security-evaluator claims traceable to source identity, version
boundaries, response content, and payment/job context, while refusing to turn
constructed or seller-supplied evidence into clearance. That is a strong
technical narrative and useful coverage.

The new Phase 5BI source-authenticated scorecard turns one of those safeguards
into a directly judgeable measurement result: on the frozen two-provider,
12-reference roster, the recorded paid/HTTP-200 accept-all baseline admits 2
known version-inapplicable references, while the exact package/version gate
displays 10 and admits 0. The measured effect is therefore a bounded 2/12
false-positive reduction (10/10 reviewed-reference precision after the gate),
reproduced by both Phase 5Y and Phase 5Z. It is evaluation material and
valuable version-aware coverage, not general security accuracy, recall, expert
gold, network ranking uplift or independent adoption.

The [Phase 5BL cross-source measurement audit](../data/phase5bl-cross-source-measurement-discrimination.json)
adds a new fair comparison surface across the original Truvian fixtures, the
complete 129-case Gig Guard ledger, the Phase 5BI roster and the fresh Phase
5BJ holdout. It records two source-bound contrasts against weaker policies,
ties the competent baseline 66/66, and preserves the negative verdict-only
tie. Phase 5BM remains a historical balanced contrast. The corrected Phase
5BN result is 22/24 against a 24/24 external resolver, so the current internal
measurement allocation is 38/40 rather than a perfect score. These results are
not human gold or a ranking-improvement claim.

The prior Phase 5BJ, Phase 5BI and Foundation evidence remains green in its
[fresh-provider](https://github.com/Iniwura/falsum/actions/runs/45919862041),
[scorecard](https://github.com/Iniwura/falsum/actions/runs/45919861960), and
[Foundation](https://github.com/Iniwura/falsum/actions/runs/45919862008) runs.
Measurement commit `b8db95d` is additionally verified by the [Phase 5BL
measurement workflow](https://github.com/Iniwura/falsum/actions/runs/45942955107)
and [Phase 5BK mapping workflow](https://github.com/Iniwura/falsum/actions/runs/45942955061).
Hosted action Node.js deprecation annotations are non-blocking; successful
conclusions are the authoritative results.

The 96/100 corrected result is an internal, evidence-mapped readiness assessment, not an
organizer score. The largest remaining excellence-award risks are independent
repeat integration or contribution beyond the existing reviews, a
registered/usable evaluator contribution, and external
adoption.
The official rules explicitly distinguish testnet/internal activity from
independently funded customer demand and prohibit self-dealing from being
presented as external demand.
