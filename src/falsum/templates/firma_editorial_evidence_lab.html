WHAT IS FALSUM?

Evidence over
confidence.

FALSUM is a security-review evaluation project for Telegraph's SECURITY_REVIEW_GENERAL intent. We want evaluation questions to reward answers that identify what the code actually proves, rather than what sounds convincing.

Our separate Anjoman contribution contains 20 AI-assisted preference pairs reported as Eligible in the contributor account. They are kept private. These three public learning examples are different and do not expose those questions.
